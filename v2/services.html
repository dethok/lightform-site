Services

Total solutions for light and furnishings.

From the first fixture to the last curtain, one team covers lighting, curtains and blinds, electrical work and renovation, for corporate and individual projects.

01

Lighting

Indoor and outdoor lighting from a full range of European and Chinese products, for commercial and residential spaces. The showroom also holds the Swarovski Lighting concept store.

02

Curtains & Blinds

Made-to-order curtains and blinds, with solar films, wall coverings, upholstery, Ziptrak, fabrics and window accessories. Offered since 2011.

03

Electrical Work

Electrical work to support the lighting we supply, backed by a dedicated team for installation and re-wiring.

04

Renovation Work

Renovation and refurbishment for cafés, offices, houses and apartments, drawing on years of project work with developers, architects and interior designers.

After-sales

Support after the sale.

A dedicated support team looks after installation, re-wiring, custom fabrication, maintenance and cleaning.
