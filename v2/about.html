About

Thirty years of light on Balestier Road.

Light + Form Concepts began as a lighting company in 1990. Today the showroom brings together lighting, curtains, blinds and lifestyle items under one roof, for homes and commercial spaces alike.

1990

Started as a lighting company

A full range of European and Chinese lighting, commercial to residential, indoor and outdoor.

Swarovski Lighting

Exclusive partner

A dedicated Swarovski Lighting concept store sits inside the showroom, with contemporary and classical crystal chandeliers.

2011

Blinds and curtain furnishings added

Made-to-order curtains, blinds, solar films, wall coverings, upholstery and Ziptrak.

Today

Renovation and refurbishment

Projects for cafés, offices, houses and apartments.

“We believe in aligning our goals with those of our clients and partners; providing them with unparalleled professional service, quality products and prompt delivery.”
Swarovski Lighting

A concept store inside the showroom.

Schonbek has handcrafted crystal chandeliers since 1870. Alongside it, the Crystal Palace collection brings designer collaborations to the range.

Light + Form is the exclusive Singapore partner, with a dedicated Swarovski Lighting concept store in the showroom.

Projects

From private residences to commercial buildings.

Our collaboration with developers, architects and interior designers over the years has resulted in an outstanding portfolio of lighting solutions for spaces that span private residences to commercial buildings.
