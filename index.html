About Light + Form

A 30-year-old light shop on Balestier Road.

We began as a lighting company in 1990. Today we are the exclusive partner of Swarovski Lighting, with a dedicated concept store inside the showroom, and carry a full range of European and Chinese lighting, indoor and outdoor, commercial to residential. In 2011 we added made-to-order curtains and blinds. Our projects run from cafes and offices to houses and apartments.
